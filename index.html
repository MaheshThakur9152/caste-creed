Frequently asked questions

Short answers about sizing, engraving and caring for your Creed Signet.

Is the ring solid or plated?

Solid all the way through. The gold version is solid gold and the silver version is sterling silver, so there is no surface layer to wear away.

How do I find my ring size?

Use our printable sizer or measure a ring you already wear. If the fit is off, we will resize your first ring free of charge.

What can I have engraved?

Up to 24 characters inside the band: names, dates, coordinates or a short line. We check every request before casting.

How long does it take to make?

Each ring is cast to order. Plan on about two weeks from order to dispatch, longer for engraved pieces in peak season.

Can I add a crest to the face?

Yes. Send us your crest or initials after ordering and we will return a drawing for approval before cutting.

How should I care for it?

Wipe with the soft cloth in the box. Take it off for chlorine and heavy lifting. Silver can be brought back with a polishing cloth.
